# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-12-01

This privacy policy applies to the Pausewood app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Soluraize Studio (collectively, the "Application"). Soluraize Studio is hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider".

## Data Controller Information

Soluraize Studio acts as the Data Controller responsible for the processing of your personal data.

* Name: Soluraize Studio
* Contact: soluraizestudio@gmail.com

For data protection inquiries and to exercise your GDPR rights, please contact the Data Controller using the contact information above.

## What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

An account, registered with your email address, is required to use the Application. The Application and related services process the information you supply when you register and use the Application:

* email address and account identifier;
* wishlist items you add (name, price, brand, model, links, image links, notes) and the decisions you make about them;
* your answers and notes in the reflection questions;
* your progress in the Application (experience points, coins, rewards, home items);
* your time zone and language;
* a push notification token, if you allow notifications.

We use this information to provide the Application, to keep your progress, to send you service messages (such as a sign-in or confirmation link and reminders you have enabled), and to keep the Application secure.

## Where your data is stored

Your data is stored and processed on our behalf by Supabase, which provides database and authentication hosting. Data is transmitted to Supabase over encrypted connections and is protected by access rules that allow each user to access only their own data.

## Advertising

The Application may offer optional rewarded ads (watching an ad in exchange for in-app coins). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers and usage data in accordance with its own privacy policy. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

## Payments

Optional in-app purchases (tips) are processed by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

## Crash reports

Crash reports are sent to Firebase Crashlytics (Google) only if you enable "Crash reports" in the Application settings, and you can turn this off at any time. If you turn it off, unsent reports are deleted. Reports contain technical error data (such as the error type, stack trace, device model, operating system version and a random installation identifier) and do not contain the text of your wishlist items or notes.

## Legal basis for processing your personal data

Where the GDPR applies, the Service Provider relies on the following lawful bases:

* Contract performance: processing necessary to provide the Application to you (account, wishlist, progress, service messages).
* Consent: where you have given consent, such as to crash reports, personalized advertising or push notifications. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing that occurred before withdrawal.
* Legitimate interests: maintaining network and information security and preventing fraud and abuse, provided those interests are not overridden by your data protection rights or fundamental freedoms.
* Legal obligation: to comply with laws or government requests.

## What information does the Application collect automatically?

In addition, the Application may collect certain technical information automatically, such as the type of mobile device you use, your mobile operating system and the way you use the Application. If you enable crash reports or ads, the corresponding service providers (Google) may also receive device identifiers and an IP address as described in their privacy policies.

## Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

No. The Application does not collect precise information about the location of your device.

## Does the Application use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies?

No. The Application does not use Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies to process your data or provide features.

## Automated decision-making and profiling

The Application does not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you.

## Do third parties have access to information obtained by the Application?

The Service Provider may share your information with the following third-party service providers, which have their own privacy policies:

* [Supabase](https://supabase.com/privacy) (database and authentication hosting);
* [Google Play Services and Google Play Billing](https://policies.google.com/privacy);
* [Google AdMob](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites);
* [Firebase Crashlytics](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy/).

The Service Provider may also disclose your information:

* as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process;
* when it believes in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect its rights, your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;
* to trusted service providers who work on its behalf, do not have an independent use of the information disclosed to them, and have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy policy.

Where the GDPR applies, the Service Provider enters into data processing agreements with third-party service providers that process personal data on its behalf, as required by Article 28 of the GDPR.

## International data transfers

The Service Provider or its third-party service providers may transfer personal data outside the European Economic Area (EEA). Where such transfers occur, the Service Provider uses an appropriate transfer mechanism required by GDPR Chapter V, such as adequacy decisions by the European Commission or Standard Contractual Clauses. Countries outside the EEA may not provide the same level of data protection as the EEA.

## What are my opt-out rights?

You can stop further collection of information from your mobile device by uninstalling the Application. Uninstalling does not automatically delete information that has already been transmitted to the Service Provider; to delete it, use the account deletion described below.

You can turn off crash reports and notifications in the Application settings and in your device settings at any time.

## Data retention and deletion

The Service Provider retains your data for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time directly in the Application (Profile > Delete account), or by contacting soluraizestudio@gmail.com. Deletion removes your progress, rewards, home, wishlist and waiting history and cannot be undone. Copies in technical backups are removed in the normal backup cycle.

Data we must keep to comply with the law is retained for as long as the law requires.

## How does the Application address children's privacy?

The Application is not intended for children under 16 years of age, or where a higher age of digital consent is established under applicable law. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If the Service Provider discovers that a child has provided personal information, it will delete it. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided personal information, please contact the Service Provider at soluraizestudio@gmail.com so that the necessary actions can be taken.

## How is your information kept secure?

The Service Provider is committed to safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. It implements electronic and procedural safeguards, such as encrypted connections, per-user access rules in the database and limited access to authorized persons who need it to operate, develop or improve the Application. However, no security system can prevent all potential security breaches.

## Data breach notification

In the event of a personal data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, the Service Provider will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach, as required by applicable law. Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to your rights and freedoms, the Service Provider will also notify you without undue delay.

## How will you be informed of changes to this Privacy Policy?

The Service Provider may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced by posting the updated policy with a new effective date and, where required by law, by asking for your consent. Previous versions are available on request at soluraizestudio@gmail.com.

## What are your GDPR data protection rights?

Under the GDPR, you have the following rights:

* Right of access: you can request access to your personal data.
* Right to rectification: you can request correction of inaccurate data.
* Right to erasure: you can request deletion of your personal data.
* Right to restrict processing: you can request that the Data Controller limits how it uses your data.
* Right to data portability: you can request a copy of your data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
* Right to object: you can object to processing based on legitimate interests.
* Right to withdraw consent: where processing is based on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time, by changing the settings in the Application or by contacting the Data Controller.

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local Data Protection Authority. Contact details can be found at [https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en](https://edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members_en).

If you are located in the United Kingdom, you may contact the Information Commissioner's Office at [https://ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk).

## What are your California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)?

If you are a resident of California, the CCPA and CPRA provide you with additional rights regarding your personal information:

* Right to know: you can request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information collected about you.
* Right to delete: you can request deletion of personal information collected from you, subject to certain exceptions.
* Right to correct: you can request correction of inaccurate personal information.
* Right to opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
* Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of your rights.

To exercise any of these rights, contact the Service Provider at soluraizestudio@gmail.com. The Service Provider will verify your request and respond within the timeframes required by law. You may designate an authorized agent to make a request on your behalf.

## How do you give your consent?

Where processing is based on consent, you give it by affirmatively opting in to the relevant feature or action, for example by turning on "Crash reports" in the Application settings. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

## How can you contact the Data Controller?

If you have any questions about privacy or want to exercise any of your rights, contact the Service Provider by email at soluraizestudio@gmail.com. The Service Provider will respond within one month of receiving your request, extendable by up to two months where necessary due to the complexity or volume of requests, as permitted by applicable law.
