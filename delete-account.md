# Delete your Pausewood account

This page explains how to delete your Pausewood account and the data linked to it. The app is operated by Soluraize Studio.

## Option 1: delete it in the app

1. Open Pausewood and sign in.
2. Go to **Profile**.
3. Tap **Delete account** and confirm by typing the requested word.

Your account and the data linked to it are deleted right away and cannot be restored.

## Option 2: request deletion by email

If you cannot open the app or have uninstalled it, send an email to soluraizestudio@gmail.com:

* Subject: **Delete my Pausewood account**
* Write from the email address you used to register, so we can confirm the account is yours. If you write from another address, we may ask you to confirm ownership of the account.

We will delete the account and reply to confirm, without undue delay.

## What is deleted

* Your email address and account identifier.
* Your wishlist items, notes, reflection answers, decisions, goals and progress (experience points, coins, rewards, home items).
* Your settings (time zone, language) and your push notification token.
* Your records of accepting the Terms and the Privacy Policy.

## What may remain

* Crash reports sent to Firebase Crashlytics (only if you allowed them) do not contain your name, email or wishlist content. They are deleted by the provider on its own schedule.
* Copies in technical backups are removed in the normal backup cycle.
* Data that the law requires us to keep, if any.

See the Privacy Policy for details on how data is processed. Questions: soluraizestudio@gmail.com.
